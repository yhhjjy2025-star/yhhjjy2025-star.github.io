Privacy Policy & Terms (English)

Tianxi Wannianli is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains our data practices and legal disclaimers.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tianxi Wannianli is a fully offline application providing a Chinese perpetual calendar, traditional almanac (Huangli), auspicious-date selection, and a relationship-compatibility reading feature. We DO NOT collect, upload, store, or share any of your personal data (including but not limited to: birth date, birth time, gender, and birth location) on any remote servers. All operations are processed strictly on your local device.

2. Data Storage

Only lightweight preferences (such as your chosen theme, language, and display switches) are stored locally on your device. Any birth information entered for the compatibility feature is used on-the-fly for local calculation only and is never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, data analytics tools, or user trackers. The application does not request the internet permission at all, ensuring zero risk of data tracking or privacy leaks.

4. Data Attribution

City coordinate and time-zone data used by the optional birthplace picker is derived from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

5. Disclaimer (Cultural Research & Entertainment Only)

All almanac guidance (Yi/Ji), date-selection suggestions, compatibility readings, and other outputs are provided strictly for cultural research and entertainment purposes only. The results do not possess any definitive predictive or life-guidance meaning, nor do they constitute professional financial, legal, medical, or life advice. The studio and developers assume no legal liability or responsibility for any decisions made or actions taken by users based on the application's outputs.
