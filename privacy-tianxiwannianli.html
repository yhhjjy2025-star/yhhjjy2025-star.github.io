Privacy Policy & Terms (English)

Tianxi Wannianli is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains our data practices and legal disclaimers.

1. Information Collection and Use

The perpetual calendar, traditional almanac (Huangli), auspicious-date selection and the daily fortune stick all run entirely on your device; these features collect no personal information.

Two features require an internet connection: "Today's Fortune" and "Compatibility Reading". When you use them, the birth information you enter (birth date, time and gender; optionally the lunar/solar calendar choice) is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to the Tianxi server for that one calculation only. The result is returned to your device and the birth information is discarded immediately after the calculation — it is not stored, not written to any database, and not linked to your identity. We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

To keep the service fair (each device may use these features three times a day), the app generates a random identifier on first use and sends it with each request. This identifier is not a hardware ID, is not linked to you, and only a one-way hash of it is kept on the server as a usage counter, which is automatically deleted after seven days.

2. Data Storage

Only lightweight preferences (such as your chosen theme, language, display switches and the random identifier above) are stored locally on your device. Birth information you enter is kept in memory only while the app is open and is cleared when you close it. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

3. Third-Party Services

This application does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, data analytics tools, or user trackers. The internet permission is used solely for the two features above; the calculation server is operated by Tianxi Studio (hosted on Cloudflare) and does not share data with any third party.

4. Data Attribution

City coordinate and time-zone data used by the optional birthplace picker is derived from GeoNames, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

5. Disclaimer (Cultural Research & Entertainment Only)

Tianxi Wannianli is a calendar tool based on traditional Chinese chronology and numerology formulas. All almanac guidance (Yi/Ji), date-selection suggestions, compatibility readings, and other outputs are provided strictly for cultural research and entertainment purposes only. The results do not possess any definitive predictive or life-guidance meaning, nor do they constitute professional financial, legal, medical, or life advice. The studio and developers assume no legal liability or responsibility for any decisions made or actions taken by users based on the application's outputs.
