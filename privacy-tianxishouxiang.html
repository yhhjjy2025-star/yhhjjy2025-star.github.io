Privacy Policy & Terms (English)

Tianxi Kan Shouxiang is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains our data practices and legal disclaimers.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tianxi Kan Shouxiang is a fully offline application that reads palm features from a photograph of your own hand. We DO NOT collect, upload, store, or share any of your personal data on any remote servers. Your palm photograph is analysed entirely on your own device, held in memory only for the duration of that analysis, and then discarded. It is never transmitted anywhere and is never written to our storage. The application requests no internet access permission (android.permission.INTERNET), so it is technically incapable of sending your photograph or any other data off your device. (A camera library it depends on declares ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which only allows checking whether a network exists; it grants no ability to transmit anything, and the app does not use it.)

The application contains no face recognition, performs no identity matching, and builds no biometric database of any kind.

2. Data Storage

Only lightweight in-app state (such as your remaining in-app credits and the readings you have already generated) is stored locally on your device. This data is never synchronised or uploaded. Uninstalling the application, or clearing its app data, removes all of it permanently — we cannot recover it, because we never received it.

4. Third-Party Services

This application does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, data analytics tools, or user trackers, and does not sell or share your data with anyone. When you choose to use the "share" function, the system hands the content to an application you select yourself; how that application handles the content is governed by its own privacy policy.

This application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children. As it collects no personal information at all, no such collection can occur.

5. Disclaimer (Cultural Research & Entertainment Only)

Tianxi Kan Shouxiang is based on traditional Chinese palmistry. All readings, descriptions, and suggestions it produces are provided strictly for cultural research and entertainment purposes only. The results do not possess any definitive predictive or life-guidance meaning, and do not constitute medical, health, psychological, financial, legal, or life advice. The studio and developers assume no legal liability or responsibility for any decisions made or actions taken by users based on the application's outputs.

隐私政策与免责条款（中文）

天禧看手相致力于保护您的个人隐私。本文件旨在说明我们如何处理数据及相关的法律免责声明。

1. 信息收集与使用

天禧看手相（Tianxi Kan Shouxiang）是一款纯本地运行的手相应用,通过您本人手掌的照片读取掌纹特征。我们不会、也绝不在任何远程服务器上收集、上传、存储或共享您的任何个人数据。您的手掌照片全部在您自己的设备上完成分析,仅在分析期间存在于内存中,分析结束即丢弃,不会传输到任何地方,也不会写入我们的任何存储。本应用不申请互联网访问权限(android.permission.INTERNET),因此在技术上就无法把您的照片或任何数据送出您的设备。(所依赖的相机库声明了一条 ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE,该权限仅能查询「当前有没有网络」,不具备任何传输能力,本应用也未使用它。)

本应用不含人脸识别,不做身份比对,不建立任何形式的生物特征数据库。

2. 数据存储

本应用仅在您的设备本地保存轻量的应用内状态（例如剩余的应用内点数、您已经生成过的报告）。这些数据不同步、不上传。卸载应用或清除应用数据即永久清除全部内容 —— 我们无从恢复,因为我们从未收到过它们。
