Privacy Policy & Terms (English)

Tianxi Bazi is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains our data practices and legal disclaimers.

1. Information Collection and Use

All BaZi chart calculations — city lookup, true solar time conversion and chart plotting — run entirely on your device and work offline. Only the Daily Fortune feature needs the internet: when you open it, the birth details of the chart you are viewing (birth date and time, gender and birthplace time zone; birthplace longitude only when true solar time is turned on), along with today's date and the sections you choose, are sent over an encrypted connection to the Tianxi server for a one-time calculation. The birth details themselves are not kept after the result is returned and are not linked to your identity.

2. Data Storage

Saved chart profiles (names and birth information) are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded or shared, and uninstalling the app removes them. To count daily usage, the app creates a random ID the first time you use Daily Fortune and sends it with Daily Fortune requests. It is not an advertising ID and contains no personal information. For counting and abuse prevention, the server keeps only scrambled (hashed) records derived from this ID, the birth details and your network (IP) address; they are deleted automatically after about one week.

3. Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics tools or trackers. The Tianxi servers are operated by us and reached through the Cloudflare network; your data is not sold or shared with any third party.

4. Data Sources and Attribution

City geographic data (coordinates and IANA time zones) bundled inside the app comes from GeoNames (geonames.org), used under the CC BY 4.0 license. The list of cities in mainland China is public administrative-division information, with coordinates likewise from GeoNames.

5. Disclaimer (Cultural Research & Entertainment Only)

Tianxi Bazi is a charting tool based on traditional Chinese numerology and chronological calculation formulas. All generated charts, fortune readings, texts, and contents (including the daily/monthly/yearly fortune sections and the folk-culture "Bone Weight" feature) are provided strictly for cultural research, historical study, and entertainment purposes only. The results do not possess any definitive life guidance meaning, nor do they constitute professional financial, legal, medical, or life advice. The studio and developers assume no legal liability or responsibility for any decisions made or actions taken by users based on the application's outputs.

隐私政策与免责条款（中文）

天禧八字致力于保护您的个人隐私。本文件旨在说明我们如何处理数据及相关的法律免责声明。

1. 信息收集与使用

八字排盘的全部计算（城市查询、真太阳时换算、排盘）都在您的手机本地完成，离线即可使用。只有「今日运程」需要联网：打开时，把所看命例的生辰（出生日期、时间、性别、出生地时区；开启真太阳时才另带出生地经度），连同当天日期和您选的板块，经加密连接发给天禧服务器当次计算，生辰本身算完不保存、不关联身份。

2. 数据存储

您保存的命例（名字与出生信息）只存储在本机，不上传、不共享；卸载 App 即随之清除。为统计每天的查询次数，首次使用「今日运程」时 App 生成一个随机编号，随运程请求发送，它不是广告标识，也不含任何个人信息。为计数和防刷，服务器只保存由编号、生辰与网络地址算出的打乱记录，约一周后自动删除。

3. 第三方服务

本应用不含任何第三方广告 SDK、数据分析工具或追踪器。天禧服务器由我们自己运营，经 Cloudflare 网络访问；您的数据不出售、不与任何第三方共享。

4. 数据来源与署名

App 内置的城市地理数据（经纬度、IANA 时区）来源于 GeoNames（geonames.org），依据 CC BY 4.0 授权使用。中国大陆城市名单为行政区划公开信息，坐标同样来自 GeoNames。
