Privacy Policy (English)

Tianxi Ziwei is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our practices regarding your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Tianxi Ziwei is a fully offline application designed for Ziwei Dou Shu chart plotting. We DO NOT collect, upload, store, or share any of your personal data (including but not limited to: birth date, birth time, gender, and birth location) on any remote servers. All operations are processed strictly on your local device.

2. Data Storage

All calculated data entered by the user is used solely for local algorithm processing. Data is processed on-the-fly and is either cleared after use or saved encrypted strictly within your device's local storage.

3. Third-Party Services

Version 1.0 of this application does not contain any third-party advertising SDKs, data analytics tools, or user trackers. The application does not require internet permissions to function, ensuring zero risk of data tracking or privacy leaks.

隐私政策（中文）

天禧紫微致力于保护您的个人隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明我们如何处理您的数据。

1. 信息收集与使用

天禧紫微（Tianxi Ziwei）是一款纯本地运行的紫微斗数排盘工具。我们不会、也绝不在任何远程服务器上收集、上传、存储或共享您输入的任何个人数据（包括但不限于：生辰八字、出生时间、性别、出生地点等）。所有数据处理均在您的本地设备上完成。

您输入的所有测算数据仅用于设备本地的排盘算法逻辑计算，数据随用随走，或仅加密保存在您的本地设备内存中。

本应用 1.0 版本不包含任何第三方广告 SDK、数据分析工具或网络追踪器。应用完全不请求网络权限，不存在任何隐私泄露与数据追踪风险。
